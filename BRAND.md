# RX Decision Brand System v1.0

**Status:** Approved base direction  
**Primary brand:** RX Decision  
**Product context:** clinical decision support for antimicrobial therapy  
**Visual character:** clinical, precise, restrained, modern, high-trust

## 1. Primary logo

The production logo is **RX Decision on one horizontal line**.

- `RX`: Inter ExtraBold 800, `#0F3D2E`
- `Decision`: Inter Bold 700, `#0B0B0B`
- `RX` must always remain fully readable.
- No ambiguous X/K/arrow construction.
- No symbol is inserted inside or between the letters.
- The primary brand logo is never stacked.

The phrase **EVIDENCE TO BETTER CARE** is an optional secondary lockup, not a mandatory part of the logo.

## 2. App icon

The app icon uses a separate lockup:

**RX**  
**Decision**

Master artboard: **1024 × 1024 px**.

- background: `#0F3D2E`
- RX: Inter ExtraBold 800, white
- Decision: Inter SemiBold 600, white
- “Decision” is intentionally large enough to remain readable at practical icon sizes.
- Do not bake rounded corners into the store master; platform masks apply them.

## 3. Color palette

| Role | HEX |
|---|---|
| Primary Green | `#0F3D2E` |
| Near Black | `#0B0B0B` |
| Cool Gray | `#6B7280` |
| Light Gray | `#E5E7EB` |
| White | `#FFFFFF` |

Rules:
- white/light-gray surfaces are the default;
- dark green and near-black are high-trust anchors;
- no neon green;
- no blue/purple “AI” gradients;
- gradients are avoided by default;
- red/orange are reserved for genuine clinical warning semantics, not decoration.

## 4. Typography

Family: **Inter**

- Display: ExtraBold 800
- Headings: Bold 700
- Subheads: SemiBold 600
- UI labels: Medium 500
- Body: Regular 400
- Body emphasis: Medium 500 / SemiBold 600

No random secondary fonts in social graphics.

## 5. Layout system

- generous whitespace;
- strong alignment;
- clear hierarchy;
- minimal decorative elements;
- evidence/artifact over decoration;
- one dominant idea per composition;
- simple rules/dividers rather than ornamental shapes;
- icons only when they improve comprehension.

## 6. Instagram standard

Default feed/carousel artboard: **1080 × 1350 px (4:5)**.

Mandatory:
- one main idea per slide;
- readable on a phone without zooming;
- no dense small text;
- default slide structure: headline + short support + one visual;
- usually 3–6 slides;
- do not upload extra-tall screenshots directly;
- place screenshots inside a 4:5 composition or crop safely;
- clinical details belong in the caption unless the detail itself is the story.

Historical clinical screenshots:
- preserve the original factual screenshot;
- no generative redrawing of drug names, doses, clinical values, or UI text;
- crop, frame, mask identifiers, and label historical context as needed.

## 7. Diagram style

Use:
- primary green for active/selected decision paths;
- near-black for key headings;
- cool gray for supporting context;
- light gray for neutral containers;
- simple lines/arrows;
- restrained rounded rectangles;
- no 3D;
- no glowing nodes;
- no generic neural-network decoration.

## 8. Voice inside graphics

- short;
- factual;
- clinical;
- no generic innovation slogans;
- no exaggerated “AI revolution” language;
- no claim stronger than current product evidence.

## 9. Clear space

Keep clear space around the horizontal wordmark equal to at least the approximate cap-height of the `R`.

No text, borders, icons, or image edges should enter this zone.

## 10. Minimum sizes

Digital:
- primary horizontal logo: recommended minimum width 180 px;
- tagline lockup: recommended minimum width 320 px;
- app icon: generate required platform sizes from the 1024 master.

If the tagline becomes unreadable, remove it instead of shrinking it further.

## 11. Never do this

- distort the logo;
- use a different X shape;
- recolor individual letters arbitrarily;
- add a medical cross, pill, DNA helix, heartbeat line, or AI sparkle into the logo;
- apply drop shadows to the core wordmark;
- regenerate the production logo with an image model;
- use a new random social style from post to post.

## 12. Production source of truth

Use the exported SVG/PNG files in this asset pack.

AI-generated brand boards are concept references only. They are not production logo files.

All future Content Work should reuse this Brand System v1 rather than inventing a new visual style.
