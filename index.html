The origin

RX Decision did not
start with AI

It grew from clinical pharmacology practice and work on antimicrobial stewardship. An earlier Telegram-based decision-support system was a first step in turning clinical knowledge into explicit rules

RX Decision takes that work forward, with a focus on making the sources, reasoning and limits behind antimicrobial recommendations available for review

The principles guiding development

  1. 01

    Source-linked
    clinical logic

    Identifiable sources, clear clinical context and documented limits for the rules behind a recommendation

  2. 02

    Explicit and auditable
    recommendations

    A visible path from clinical inputs to a recommendation, with reasoning that can be examined

  3. 03

    Clinical decisions remain
    with the physician

    The physician assesses applicability, considers the patient’s circumstances and makes the final decision
